    Frequently asked questions

    Quick answers about quality, privacy, limits and compatibility.

    Is this PDF compressor really free?
    Yes. There is no signup, watermark or daily limit. The tool runs in your browser, so the only practical limit is the memory of your own device.
    Are my PDFs uploaded to a server?
    No. Compression happens inside your browser tab using JavaScript libraries, and your documents are never transmitted. You can confirm this by watching the Network tab in your browser's developer tools while you compress a file.
    How much smaller will my PDF get?
    It depends on the content. Scanned and photo-heavy PDFs often shrink dramatically, while text-only PDFs are already small and may not shrink at all. The size guard keeps your original if the result would be larger.
    Will compression reduce the quality of my PDF?
    Compression is lossy, so some detail is traded for size. The Balanced preset keeps text readable at normal screen and print sizes, High Quality preserves more detail, and you can compare the sizes and adjust before downloading.
    Does grayscale mode really save space?
    Usually, yes, especially for scanned documents and text-heavy pages where colour adds no value. Removing colour and cleaning up the paper background lets each page compress more efficiently. Savings vary by document.
    Can I compress several PDFs at once?
    Yes. Add as many PDFs as your device can handle, compress them in one run, and use Download All as ZIP to save every result in a single archive.
    Is there a maximum file size?
    We do not impose a fixed limit. Files up to tens of megabytes work well on most devices, while very large files of hundreds of megabytes are better handled in a desktop browser with plenty of free memory.
    Will the text in my PDF stay selectable and searchable?
    No. To achieve large savings, each page is re-rendered as a compressed image, which flattens selectable text. If you need searchable text, keep your original, or let the size guard apply lossless optimization when that is enough.
    Can I compress a password-protected PDF?
    Not directly. If you are authorized to open the file, remove the password in your PDF viewer, save an unlocked copy, and add that copy to the compressor.
    Does it work on phones and tablets?
    Yes. It works in current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on desktop, Android and iOS. Large documents are faster on a desktop, and WebP image export falls back to JPG in browsers that cannot encode WebP.
